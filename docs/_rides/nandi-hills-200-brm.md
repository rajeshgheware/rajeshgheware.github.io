faq:
  - q: What is a BRM?
    a: >-
      A BRM (Brevet de Randonneurs Mondiaux) is a long-distance, self-supported ride over a set route with checkpoints
      and a time limit. It is not a race. In India, BRMs run under Audax India Randonneurs, which represents the Audax
      Club Parisien (ACP).
  - q: What are the BRM time limits?
    a: >-
      Under ACP rules: 13 h 30 min for 200 km, 20 h for 300 km, 27 h for 400 km and 40 h for 600 km, counted from the
      start, including all stops.
  - q: How hard is the Nandi Hills climb by bike?
    a: >-
      It is the best-known climb near Bengaluru: a steady road with hairpins to the top at about 1,480 m. On a 200 km
      BRM it comes with plenty of flat kilometres either side, so pacing the climb matters.

**209 km, 1,489 m of climbing, 12.4 hours of moving time.** The photo is from the top of Nandi Hills at 10:37 am.
