faq:
  - q: What is a BRM?
    a: >-
      A BRM (Brevet de Randonneurs Mondiaux) is a long-distance, self-supported ride over a set route with checkpoints
      and a time limit. It is not a race. In India, BRMs run under Audax India Randonneurs, which represents the Audax
      Club Parisien (ACP).
  - q: What are the BRM time limits?
    a: >-
      Under ACP rules: 13 h 30 min for 200 km, 20 h for 300 km, 27 h for 400 km and 40 h for 600 km, counted from the
      start, including all stops.
  - q: What does LF mean in BRM results?
    a: >-
      LF means late finish: the rider completed the route but after the time limit (13 h 30 min for a 200 km BRM), so
      the brevet is not validated. My Nandi Hills 200 in January 2025 is recorded as LF.
  - q: How hard is the Nandi Hills climb by bike?
    a: >-
      It is the best-known climb near Bengaluru: a steady road with hairpins to the top at about 1,480 m. On a 200 km
      BRM it comes with plenty of flat kilometres either side, so pacing the climb matters.

## My ride: 4 January 2025

**209 km, 1,489 m of climbing, 12.4 hours of moving time, and a late finish.** The photo is from the top of Nandi Hills
at 10:37 am.

- **Morning to midday:** the ride went well.
- **After lunch:** I had a flat tyre and spent about 30 minutes fixing it.
- **Late afternoon and evening:** my energy was low.
- **After sunset:** the cool breeze brought my speed and energy back. On the way I ate a banana and peanut chikki,
  which gave me enough energy to complete the ride.

I completed the full route, but a little after the event's 13.5-hour limit, so the official Audax India result is
**LF (late finish)** and the brevet doesn't count as validated. Moving time was 12.4 hours; the 30-minute puncture
and the slow afternoon used up the margin. Compare my [Puttaparthi 400](/rides/puttaparthi-400-brm/), where steady
fuelling through the day kept my energy up for 27 hours.

This was my only long ride in the first half of 2025. Looking back, that thin year of riding is part of why I had so
little endurance in reserve on [Kang Yatse 2](/treks/kang-yatse-2/) that September. See
[cycling as an endurance base for trekking](/rides/guides/cycling-for-trekkers/).
