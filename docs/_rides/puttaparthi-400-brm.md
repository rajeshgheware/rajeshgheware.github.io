## My ride: 22 July 2023

This is the longest ride in my Strava log: **405.4 km with 2,422 m of climbing, finished in 27 hours without any
sleep or naps** (23.0 hours of moving time). It came
two months after my first 200 km BRM ([Minchina Ota](/rides/minchina-ota-200-brm/)), in the biggest training year I've
had on the bike.

That summer of riding is also why I went into [Bodhpathri Lakes](/treks/bodhpathri-lakes/) in August–September 2023 with
plenty of endurance. I've written up the comparison in
[cycling as an endurance base for trekking](/rides/guides/cycling-for-trekkers/).

### My fuel: honey and electrolytes

A 400 km brevet is as much an eating and drinking problem as a riding one. Most of what I knew about food, nutrition
and hydration for this ride came from [Dr Andy Galpin's YouTube videos](https://www.youtube.com/@drandygalpin).

The idea that changed my fuelling: **your gut absorbs glucose and fructose through two separate transport channels.**
A fuel that contains both sugars can deliver more energy per hour than either one alone. Honey is naturally a mix of
glucose and fructose.

So my "power kicks" through the ride were **honey mixed with Enerzal** (an electrolyte drink), for sugar and salts in
the same bottle. It worked better than I'd hoped. I rode through the night with no sleep or naps and finished the
400 km in 27 hours.

Real food mattered too. Alongside the honey and electrolytes, **curd rice was one of the foods that made a big
difference** on this ride.

*Fuelling needs vary from person to person. If you have diabetes or another medical condition, plan your ride
nutrition with your doctor.*
