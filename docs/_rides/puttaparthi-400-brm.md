faq:
  - q: What is a BRM?
    a: >-
      A BRM (Brevet de Randonneurs Mondiaux) is a long-distance, self-supported ride over a set route with checkpoints
      and a time limit. It is not a race. In India, BRMs run under Audax India Randonneurs, which represents the Audax
      Club Parisien (ACP).
  - q: What are the BRM time limits?
    a: >-
      Under ACP rules: 13 h 30 min for 200 km, 20 h for 300 km, 27 h for 400 km and 40 h for 600 km, counted from the
      start, including all stops.
  - q: How long does a 400 km BRM take?
    a: >-
      The limit is 27 hours. My moving time was 23 hours for 405 km, so most of the margin goes on checkpoint and food
      stops, and riding through the night is part of it.

## My ride: 22 July 2023

This is the longest ride in my Strava log: **405.4 km with 2,422 m of climbing in 23.0 hours of moving time.** It came
two months after my first 200 km BRM ([Minchina Ota](/rides/minchina-ota-200-brm/)), in the biggest training year I've
had on the bike.

That summer of riding is also why I went into [Bodhpathri Lakes](/treks/bodhpathri-lakes/) in August–September 2023 with
plenty of endurance. I've written up the comparison in
[cycling as an endurance base for trekking](/rides/guides/cycling-for-trekkers/).

### Where my fuelling knowledge came from

A 400 km brevet is as much an eating and drinking problem as a riding one. Most of what I knew about food, nutrition
and hydration for this ride came from [Dr Andy Galpin's YouTube videos](https://www.youtube.com/@drandygalpin).
