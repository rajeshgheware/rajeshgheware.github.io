## About the Tour of Karnataka

TOK, the [Tour of Karnataka](https://tourofkarnataka.com/), is a multi-day cycling tour held every November, with
riders split into batches. On the format the organisers publish for recent editions, riders take the TOK bus from
Bengaluru to the start, ride four days between hotel stops, and take the bus back.

## My ride: 26–29 Nov 2025

**437 km in four days**, starting in Sagara and heading through Shivamogga to Chikkamagaluru on the first two days. This was my biggest block of riding in 2025, two months after [Kang Yatse 2](/treks/kang-yatse-2/).
