## Overview

The trail climbs from the Pokhara side through Ghandruk or Jhinu and Chhomrong, then up the Modi Khola gorge past
Bamboo, Dovan and Deurali to Machapuchare Base Camp (about 3,700 m). It ends at Annapurna Base Camp (4,130 m),
inside a full circle of high peaks.

## Getting there

Kathmandu → Pokhara by a short flight or a long bus or jeep ride, then a drive to the road head. New roads keep
shortening the walk, which is why published distances vary so much.
