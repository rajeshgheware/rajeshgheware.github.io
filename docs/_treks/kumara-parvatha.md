## Why it's on my list

Kumara Parvatha is the trek Bengaluru trekkers point to when they mean *hard*. The trail starts near sea level at
Kukke Subramanya and climbs to the second-highest peak in Kodagu. It goes through dense shola forest, then
open grassland ridges, past Shesha Parvatha, to the summit at 1,712 m. After high-altitude Himalayan climbs, I want a hard Western Ghats
climb close to home. This is it.

## Getting there from Bengaluru

- **Bus:** KSRTC runs overnight buses from Bengaluru to Kukke Subramanya (about 280 km). You arrive early in the
  morning, ready to start.
- **Train:** Subrahmanya Road station is on the Bengaluru–Mangaluru line, a short taxi or bus ride from Kukke town.
- **Car:** It's a long drive through Hassan and Sakleshpur. Park in Kukke town.

## The route (Kukke Subramanya side)

The figures below are typical values from published guides. I'll replace them with my own timings after the trek.

| Stage | Distance (approx.) | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| Kukke temple → Bhattara Mane (Girigadde) | 5–6 km | Steep forest climb, the hardest stretch on fresh legs. Traditional meal stop |
| Bhattara Mane → Forest check post | ~1 km | Permit check |
| Check post → Kallu Mandapa | 2–3 km | Forest gives way to grassland. Last reliable water source in most seasons |
| Kallu Mandapa → Shesha Parvatha | 1–2 km | Steep and exposed, with big views |
| Shesha Parvatha → Kumara Parvatha summit | 2–3 km | Down into a forest saddle, then the final rocky push |

You come down the same way. It is hard on the knees, so trekking poles help.

## Water, food and stay

- **Water:** Carry at least 3 litres from Kukke. Streams on the upper trail are seasonal, and some are dry by February.
- **Food:** Bhattara Mane has traditionally served simple meals to trekkers. Still, carry enough snacks and
  lunch for the whole day.
- **Stay:** Kukke has plenty of lodges and temple guest houses for the night before or after. Overnight stays on the
  trail depend on current Forest Department rules, so check them when you book.

## Permits and booking

Book on the [Aranya Vihaara portal](https://aranyavihaara.karnataka.gov.in). Sources report that slots open about
15 days ahead, cost about ₹350 + GST per person and have a daily cap. Weekends in the peak season sell out.
Carry the ID you booked with. *Check all of this on the portal, because it changes.*

## Packing list

- Trek shoes with good grip (broken in), plus sandals for the bus
- 3 L water capacity, ORS/electrolytes
- Trekking poles (for the descent)
- Light rain shell, warm layer for the summit wind, cap, sunscreen
- Headlamp with spare batteries (for an early start or late finish)
- Leech socks or salt if you go near the monsoon
- Basic first aid: blister kit, crepe bandage, painkillers
- Pack out all plastic. It's a wildlife sanctuary.
