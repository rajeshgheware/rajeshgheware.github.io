  Kudremukh Peak (about 1,894 m) is one of the highest peaks in Karnataka, in Kudremukh National Park. It's a long but
  steady day hike of about 16–22 km round trip from Mullodi, across rolling grassland with streams. A licensed guide

## Overview

Kudremukh means "horse face", after the shape of the peak seen from one side. From Mullodi the trail crosses rolling
grassland with forest in the valleys, several streams and a final climb to the summit.

## Getting there

Get to Kalasa or Kudremukh town, then take a jeep from Balegal to Mullodi. The last 6 km is jeep-only. Homestays in
Mullodi make an early start easy.
