## Overview

The trail climbs from Kakkabe past the 18th-century Nalknad Palace, through coffee plantations and forest, then on
open grassland to the summit ridge. On a clear day you can see across Kodagu and towards the coast.

## Getting there

Drive or take a bus to Virajpet or Madikeri, then go on to Kakkabe. Coorg homestays make it an easy weekend.
