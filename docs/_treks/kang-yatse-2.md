## Overview

Kang Yatse 2 rises above the Markha Valley in Hemis National Park. The approach follows one of Ladakh's classic
valley treks, through villages, river crossings and high pastures, to a base camp below the peak. Summit day starts
around midnight.

## Getting there and acclimatisation

Fly into Leh (3,500 m) and do nothing strenuous for the first 2–3 days. This is the part people most often cut
short, and it is the part that decides summit day.

## Route at a glance

The approach goes up the Markha Valley to Nimaling, then to Kang Yatse base camp, followed by summit day. The exit
is usually over Kongmaru La (about 5,200 m) to Shang Sumdo or Chogdo. The exact camps depend on the operator.

## My trek: 6–13 September 2025

I didn't make the summit. On summit day, severe lower back pain and a lack of physical energy made me turn back
before the top. On a 6,000 m peak, turning around when your body says no is the right call.
