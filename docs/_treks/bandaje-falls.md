## Overview

There are two common approaches. One climbs from Valambra near Ujire through dense forest to the top of the falls.
The other walks the ridge from Ballalarayana Durga fort (about 3.5–4 km from the fort to the falls). Many groups
combine them as a two-day crossover.

## Getting there

Take an overnight bus from Bengaluru to Ujire or Dharmasthala, then go on locally to Valambra.
