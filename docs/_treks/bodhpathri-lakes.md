## Overview

Day one climbs from Doodhpathri through Himalayan pine and fir forest. After that the trek is mostly open meadow,
with streams, shepherd huts and grazing horses, and it climbs to a chain of high lakes beneath the Pir Panjal peaks.
It's a newer route, so camps are quiet compared with the big-name Kashmir treks.

## Getting there

Fly to Srinagar. Most groups spend a night there, often on a Dal Lake houseboat, before driving to Doodhpathri.
