## Overview

Kotagiri sits at about 1,800–2,000 m on the eastern edge of the Nilgiris. Over three days we walked tea-estate trails,
climbed to shola-covered ridges and ended at a waterfall. We also saw a gaur (Indian bison) grazing in the tea bushes.

## Getting there

From Bengaluru, either drive via Mysuru and Ooty or go via Coimbatore and up the Mettupalayam ghat road. Kotagiri is
about 30 km from Ooty.
