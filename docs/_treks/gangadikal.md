## Overview

The trail starts near Kudremukh town, crosses streams and shola patches, then climbs grassy slopes to the summit
board. On a clear day you can see Kudremukh Peak and the ridges running towards the coast.

## Getting there

Drive or take a bus to Kalasa or Kudremukh via Chikkamagaluru. Homestays around Samse and Kalasa make an early start
easy.
